## What the prior attempts established

The [three-input join attempt](native-multiway-join-2026-10-20.md) showed large
finite target gains from checking all relationships before collecting payload,
but its final integration missed a control in one cohort. The
[weighted-search attempt](native-learned-index-decision-2026-10-20.md) showed
smaller finite gains from bounded prediction with exact correction, but also
missed controls in one cohort. Both remain not retained.

The failing controls already bypassed their candidate algorithms. Their cause
was not isolated, so narrower admission alone is not a demonstrated repair.
Do not rerun either unchanged candidate, relax its gates, replace a cohort or
reinterpret its favorable targets as acceptance. Keep their exactness fixtures,
failure records, restored-runtime checks and reusable implementation ideas.

## Cyclic existence joins

Owner: `PERF-10.multiway_joins`, with PERF-03 ownership and PERF-10/12 public
route and correctness obligations.

The [October 21 decision](native-cyclic-existence-intake-2026-10-21.md) retains
the finite automatic strategy. All four intermediate-row opportunities, both
complete cohorts (520 workflows, 1,300 native calls), every frozen control and
actual-work/ownership tests pass. Final native/workspace/CLI checks, 5,930 public
cases, 115 installed-package tests, Full43 and 18 format cohorts also pass.
The replayable packet preserves failures and corrections. This finite result
does not close the broader multiway owner or authorize publication.

Investigate an explicit semi-join shape in which an outer row needs only one
complete witness across two related inner inputs. For example, R(a,b) qualifies
when some S(b,c) and T(c,a) satisfy all three equalities. A native probe can
potentially test those equalities and stop at the first complete witness before
constructing inner pair payloads. Each qualifying R row is emitted once in
original R order; distinct duplicate R rows remain distinct. Inner duplicate
multiplicity cannot multiply the output of this existence operation. NULL keys
retain the existing equality and semi-join semantics.

This differs from the previous full three-input inner join, which had to emit
every matching R/S/T product. Existing binary semi/anti joins already
short-circuit matching right rows. The new investigation must demonstrate
additional avoided work across the cyclic relationship; reproducing that
existing binary optimization is not a result.

Initial scope is pure immutable memory sources, admitted exact integer keys,
left-only output and an explicit inner-join child. First verify that this plan
shape is expressible and selected through the shared public/native route. Keep
other predicates, effectful sources, full join products, arbitrary graph
traversal and new spill behavior outside this finite admission. Check the pinned
Vortex provider and current ShardLoom key/index/selection owners before choosing
an implementation.

Before timing, independently specify complete outputs for abundant, rare and
absent witnesses; skew, duplicates, NULLs, empty and tiny inputs; ordinary
two-input semi/anti, star-shaped and dense controls. Include payload widths,
projection aliases, output batch boundaries and repeated use. Count actual
candidate visits, complete-witness exits and payload rows gathered. Charge
index construction, every admitted owner and final drain; prove cancellation,
consumer failure, denial and exact cleanup. A dedicated future protocol must
freeze all generators, controls, numerical gates, source/build identity and
both complete cohorts before candidate implementation or timing.

## Reusable weighted sampling preparation

Owner: `PERF-10.learned_indexes`, with `PERF-02.physical_cache` and PERF-03
generation, credit and lifetime obligations. Existing PERF-06 pressure behavior
remains binding.

Investigate retaining only a small exact-correction model across repeated calls
on the same immutable prepared weighted-replacement plan. The previous model
recalibrated on every execution. Amortizing that calibration is a different
mechanism, but its construction, lookup, identity checks, retained credits and
teardown must all earn their cost.

Begin with a single prepared object over immutable native memory and its bound
deterministic scan/weight/order semantics. No cross-plan or cross-session reuse
is admitted by this note. Current prepared relational execution deliberately
creates fresh operator state, so a retained model needs a new explicit owner,
bounded credit and release contract. A model must own scalar metadata rather
than borrowed pointers into a finished execution's population. Source content,
bound plan, predicate, weight expression, types, candidate order and numerical
semantics must determine validity. Row count and total weight alone are not an
identity proof.

The population and its payload can remain execution-local. Retaining those
larger structures, adding a second CDF, sharing across independently prepared
plans, or extending to changing files and one-shot streams requires a separate
justification and must not be smuggled into a metadata-reuse experiment. Keep
ordinary exact search when reuse or bounded prediction is unproved; this is
the existing ShardLoom-native algorithm, never an external-engine fallback.

Before timing, verify every exact lower-bound boundary and neighboring value,
rounded duplicates, tiny/large finite totals and full seeded output. Include
fresh and repeated prepared calls, changed plans and source values with equal
counts/totals, rejected calibration, few draws, small populations and unweighted
controls. Prove that concurrent calls, cancellation, failed output, pressure,
reclaim and prepared teardown cannot publish or retain invalid state. Measure
actual calibration/reuse counts and all retained bytes. A prospective protocol
must fix the call counts, independent source/result fixtures, break-even and
single-call controls, numerical gates, fresh compiled identities and both
complete cohorts before candidate implementation or timing.

## Shared decision discipline

Use the strongest existing equivalent native route as the baseline. Include
the original failed-control shapes in the new controls wherever their semantics
apply; do not silently discard the earlier warnings. Select target and control
workloads prospectively, charge preparation and teardown, retain every sample,
and use engine clocks for routine query comparisons. Full values, schema,
ordering, certificates and zero final credits remain mandatory.

Record a finite retained, not-retained, rejected or evidenced prerequisite
decision for each follow-up. A retained implementation needs ordinary public
selection, relevant native/workspace/package acceptance and applicable PERF-12
evidence after its final integration. Keep positive microbenchmarks separate
from that acceptance. No broad multiway, sampling, remote, platform, release or
competitive claim follows from adding these investigations.
