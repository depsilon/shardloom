# Declined per-measure metadata experiment

Run `python3 -I -B verify.py` with Python 3.13 or newer. Keep every archive
part and the manifest together. Verification checks transport and all member
identities, then replays 1,274 complete results and 40 prepared lifecycle
operations from the original requests, schemas and independent references.
It verifies source-bound test inventories and recomputes the frozen decision.
No native engine, competitor, subprocess or fresh query is executed.

The candidate is dropped. Complete results, resource limits, native execution
and cleanup passed. The required requested-read reduction was not demonstrated,
and the complete 637-case fresh engine total increased from 11.827542699 to
11.858516037 seconds. The lifecycle total improved from 0.197566507 to
0.103567627 seconds; that does not override either failed acceptance gate.
The original replayer status remains incomplete_or_invalid_evidence and its
decision remains not_admitted. The separate disposition records the decision
to drop this experiment, not a claim of a valid performance acceptance.

The packet includes complete raw ledgers, all 278 individual fresh regressions,
references, exact candidate source and patch, all failed validation receipts and
logs, 27 offline negative controls and their explicitly synthetic gate fixture,
and source/provider observations for every new case. The 343 exact accepted
baseline observations are reused once; other calls execute once. Cache state
was uncontrolled. Read bytes are completed requested ranges including repeats
and coalescing gaps, not unique bytes or device I/O. Scalar measure-update
invocations were not dynamically counted.

Large data and executable/runtime/environment payloads are represented by their
original identities and pre/post-run receipts. Portable replay does not reopen
those payloads or repeat reference generation. It preserves the original source
paths and generations as historical provenance, without requiring those paths
on the replay machine. No publication or broader queue completion is implied.
